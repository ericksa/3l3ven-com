## How to Book

1. **Check Availability**: View the [Schedule](/schedule) to see upcoming dates
2. **Contact**: Send a message with your event details
3. **Deposit**: 50% deposit required to secure date
4. **Final Payment**: Balance due 48 hours before event

## Event Details Needed

When booking, please provide:
- Event date and time
- Venue name and address
- Expected guest count
- Music preferences
- Special requests
